# Final Light of the West 1.0

A total conversion for Crusader Kings II based around late Antiquity. There is only one start date as of now:  2 February 476

## What you need

- Crusader Kings II, version 3.3.5.1.
- The Historical Immersion Project, the Frosty3 release of 10 July 2020, installed with **SWMH** and **EMF** selected. HIP is not included in this download. Get it from the link above.

## Install

1. Install HIP first, following the instructions on its page on the Paradox Forum.
2. Download `FinalLightOfTheWest.zip` from this release.
3. Unzip it into `Documents\Paradox Interactive\Crusader Kings II\mod`. You should end up with two folders and two `.mod` files beside HIP's:
   - `FinalLightOfTheWest` and `FinalLightOfTheWest.mod`
   - `FinalLightOfTheWestMusic` and `FinalLightOfTheWestMusic.mod`
4. Start the game. In the launcher, tick **HIP - Historical Immersion Project** and **Final Light of the West**. Tick **Final Light of the West Music** as well if you want the music. It is optional.
5. Start a **new game**. Old saves will not work.

If the main menu shows the title "Final Light of the West" at the top left, the mod has loaded.

## What is in it

- **The world of 476.** Rulers, realms, cultures and faiths across the whole map, with period names for places, realms and families.
- **The fall of the West.** The federates' demand, Odoacer, Nepos in Dalmatia, and a moment of destiny for some twenty rulers and peoples, with wars of reconquest and migration.
- **Governments and laws.** Twenty-two new forms of rule beside the Roman imperial government, each with its own laws, obligations and ways of paying an army:
  - *The Roman world:* Military Command, Senatorial Lordship, Episcopal Lordship, Successor Kingship.
  - *The barbarian kingdoms:* Dual Kingdom, War-band Kingship, Patrimonial Kingdom, Ducal Kingdom, Conciliar Kingdom.
  - *Persia, Armenia and the East:* Eranshahr, Nakharar Principality, Tributary Overlordship, Mountain Kingdom, Oasis Kingdom.
  - *Arabia and Africa:* Phylarchate, Sanctuary City, Clan Confederacy, Rule of the Amghar, Kingship of the Kaya Maghan.
  - *The north and the steppe:* Clientship Kingship, Forest Chieftaincy, Steppe Khaganate.
- **Faiths.** Thirty faiths with their own rules, holy sites and events, including the Samaritans and the old religions of the Basques, the Circassians and the Horn of Africa.
- **Peoples.** A battle tactic for every people, customs as decisions, and sworn bands in place of holy orders.
- **Ruling.** The Burden of Rule, legitimacy, imperial decay, monuments, societies, and several hundred new events.

## Known limits

- This is a first release. Expect rough edges, and please report what you find on my discord.
- Many names and details rest on thin historical evidence. 
- Some regions far from the Roman world may carry inaccurate province names.
- The mod is built with one HIP setup (SWMH with EMF). Other HIP combinations are not supported.
